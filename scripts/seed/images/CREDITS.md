records open real images instead of a broken link. Test data only.

## Avatars

`avatar-1.png` … `avatar-20.png`: the [Notionists](https://www.dicebear.com/styles/notionists/) style by
Zoish, through DiceBear, licensed **CC0 1.0** — drawn people rather than photographs, because a
real person's face on a made-up employee or patient would be a real person's face on a lie.

## Drawn here

`signature.png` is drawn by a script. The documents (ID, certificates, letters) are generated by
the seed step as PDFs marked **SAMPLE — TEST DATA**, and are not stored here.
